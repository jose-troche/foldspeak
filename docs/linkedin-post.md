P04637.

That's the scientific name for the protein that guards your DNA.

It's one of 200 million+ protein shapes predicted by AI, work that shared a Nobel Prize. The results are free for anyone on Earth.

Almost nobody can read them.

So I built foldspeak: type "insulin," "hemoglobin," or "p53" and get:

🧬 The 3D shape, colored by how confident the AI was

🔍 The parts that matter, highlighted

💬 A plain-English explanation at your level (curious, student, or expert)

📎 A source for every single claim

The twist: the AI never answers from memory.

It first pulls the facts from trusted scientific databases. Then the AI only explains them. It has to cite its sources, and when the records don't cover something, it says "the records don't cover this" instead of making something up.

That one rule changes everything:
→ Fewer hallucinations

→ A small, cheap model is enough

→ Anyone can check the receipts

It runs for $0. No login. No ads.

Try it: https://foldspeak.troche.workers.dev

And the idea isn't really about biology. It's about medical records, contracts, pension statements, tax rules, company manuals: anywhere knowledge is public but unreadable.

The fix isn't a bigger AI. It's an AI that explains trusted sources and is honest about the gaps.
